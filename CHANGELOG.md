### Initial source publication

- Native macOS floating panel with category selection, level explanations,
  light/dark appearance, and saved panel geometry.
- Daily, session, historical-event, failed-rally, support-hold, and five-minute
  trendline detection.
- Selected-pane drawing ownership and timeframe/session restoration.
- Local immutable records of completed Draw/Clear operations.
- Pinned published TradingView MCP dependency and a single setup command for
  the panel and TradingView Debug launcher.
- Offline unit/regression tests and public installation, development, security,
  and attribution documentation.
