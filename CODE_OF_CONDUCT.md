# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces. Give technical feedback constructively, allow room for differing
experience and viewpoints, and focus criticism on the work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, and sharing another
person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Do not post
account details, credentials, or private chart records.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict
participation to protect the community. Apply these expectations consistently.

To report a conduct concern, request a private contact channel from a maintainer
without posting the underlying private details publicly. Security vulnerabilities
follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
